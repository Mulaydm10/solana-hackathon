The Challenge

Build a working MVP (Minimum Viable Product) that uses at least one Solana feature. All backgrounds are welcome — no previous blockchain experience required. AI tools are encouraged.

  • Find a real problem and explain who you're solving it for.
  • Build a working prototype using at least one Solana feature (payments, digital ownership, or an existing Solana application).
  • Make your product easy for its intended users to understand and use.
  • Explain what makes your idea useful and how you could reach your first users.
Key insight: "Start small — one useful feature that works is enough for a first prototype. You can use existing tools and building blocks — you don't have to create everything from scratch."

Judging Criteria

  • A useful idea: Does it solve a clear problem?
  • A working prototype: Can you demonstrate the core idea?
  • A clear role for Solana: Does using Solana help the product?
  • Potential to grow: Who would use it, and why?
