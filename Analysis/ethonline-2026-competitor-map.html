About "ETHGlobal Switzerland"

There was no ETHGlobal hackathon in Switzerland in 2026. The in-person events were Cannes, New York, Lisbon, Tokyo and Mumbai, and the only Swiss ETHGlobal listing is a 2025 meetup in Zug. The "Switzerland" project is ours: Capability Descent (repo Mulaydm10/ehl-switzerland-hackathon). It was submitted to ETHOnline 2026, ETHGlobal's online hackathon, and won no prize and was not a finalist.

This corrects our 1 Oct research note, which said no winners had been published. Results appear as prize badges on each project page and were announced in the finale stream.

Overlap with our idea

Projects similar to ours

A keyword pass flagged 324 of the 812 projects. Reviewed by hand, the close ones fall into three groups. Gold chips mark sponsor prizes and teal chips mark finalists.

A · Spending caps and revocation

Our core mechanism, and the most crowded category of the event. Projects that won did it through a sponsor integration (Ledger, Bazantic, ENS) or a business framing. A cap on its own did not win.

B · Agents buying data per query

Our chosen WHU use case. It produced two finalists (OpenBook, OnchainRouter) and Hedera prize winners (Carpool, Turnstile). Judges liked it, but it is not an empty space.

C · Paying only for what was delivered

Escrow, refunds and checks on the response before money is released. None of these won on that alone. OpenBook won by building refunds into a clear data-buyer story.

Our reading

What this means for the WHU entry

  1. Caps and revocation alone are now common. About 60 teams built them, ours included, and ours did not place. This matches the WHU mentor's advice to apply the mechanism to one specific use case.
  2. Our use case has been tested and it did well. Agents buying data and research is what OpenBook, OnchainRouter, Carpool and Turnstile did. That shows judges respond to it, but we cannot claim nobody has built it.
  3. Two gaps none of the winners cover (from their descriptions; verify before pitching):
    • Charging each purchase to a client or project, for consulting and VC billing. Cordon and Bursar track budgets per task or per team, but none produces a per-client invoice.
    • Solana. ETHOnline was EVM and Hedera only, and the WHU jury asks for a clear role for Solana.
  4. Ideas worth borrowing: OpenBook's pitch (market size plus "stale data, no refund"), Carpool's price that drops as research ages and its refund window, OnchainRouter's "no answer, no charge", and Cordon's shared budget across sub-agents as the demo story.
  5. A business framing gets attention. Novi Corpus reached the finals by setting up a legal company for each agent. A real-world buyer, such as a consulting firm, is a stronger pitch than a crypto-native user.

Not reviewed yet: the ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 finalists (25–27 Sep), which include Omamorei, an agent payment firewall.

Method and sources

The project list comes from the ETHGlobal showcase, scraped on 2 Oct 2026 (812 projects parsed). Each winner's page was read for its "Winner of" section. The finalists and the prize split come from the auto-captions of the ETHOnline 2026 Finale, so a few spoken amounts may be misheard. The A/B/C grouping was done by hand from taglines and descriptions. No LinkedIn posts listing the 2026 results were found.
