Procurement Layer

An AI turns a request into deal terms. A Solana program holds the money and enforces them.

A buyer, a person or an AI agent, says what it needs. The AI picks a listed service and fills in an audited deal template. The escrow program on Solana checks the buyer's spending policy, holds the payment, takes a stake from the seller, and pays only for a delivery that matches the terms. Missed deadlines, bad deliveries and silence all have a rule, and the program applies it.

Open risks

  • Shared network. The Mac sits on a shared campus IP, so the public devnet RPC rate-limits it; the Omen does not hit this.
  • One verifier key. A single key decides challenges. That is a trust assumption; several independent verifiers are the upgrade path.
  • Keys on machines. Demo, verifier and upgrade keys are files on the Mac and the Omen. Fine for devnet, not for real money.
  • Test money. All "USDC" is a test token we mint; nothing has real value.

Flow diagram

A deal from request to settlement

Every branch below is enforced by the program. Refusals are normal answers with a reason code, and no money moves when something is refused.

flowchart TD
  Ask["Buyer asks in plain words"] --> Find["AI finds a listed service
and drafts the terms"]
  Find --> Check{"Terms within budget?"}
  Check -- "no" --> R1["Refused: OVER_BUDGET
nothing moves"]
  Check -- "yes" --> Lock["Lock: program checks the buyer policy
and escrows the order"]
  Lock -- "policy fails" --> R2["Refused by Solana:
OverMaxPrice · SellerNotAllowed ·
OverPeriodBudget · ApprovalRequired"]
  Lock --> Open(["Open"])
  Open -- "buyer cancels" --> Cancelled(["Cancelled · full refund"])
  Open -- "seller accepts, posts stake" --> Funded(["Funded"])
  Funded -- "deadline passes" --> Slash1(["Refunded · stake slashed to buyer"])
  Funded -- "seller submits hash + invoice
(invoice within tolerance)" --> Delivered(["Delivered"])
  Delivered -- "buyer releases that exact delivery" --> Released(["Released · seller paid, stake back"])
  Delivered -- "buyer silent past review window" --> Claimed(["Claimed · seller paid"])
  Delivered -- "buyer challenges, posts bond" --> Challenged(["Challenged"])
  Challenged -- "verifier: delivery OK" --> Pass(["VerifiedPass · seller paid + bond"])
  Challenged -- "verifier: delivery fails" --> Fail(["VerifiedFail · buyer refunded + stake"])
  Challenged -- "no verdict in time" --> NoVerdict(["NoVerdict · buyer refunded, stake back"])
